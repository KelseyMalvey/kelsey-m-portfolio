About

Experience design that connects people, purpose, and performance.

I work at the intersection of customer insight, operational clarity, and brand emotion to create journeys that build trust and accelerate business outcomes.

I’m Kelsey Malvey, a customer experience strategist helping organizations turn customer signals into meaningful momentum. My work blends research, journey mapping, service design, and strategic facilitation to uncover what matters most to people and what needs to change to create better outcomes.

Whether a company is trying to improve retention, simplify service, or align teams around a better customer promise, I help bring clarity to the path forward.

I believe exceptional experiences are built when strategy is grounded in empathy and execution is anchored in action. That means listening deeply, designing simply, and helping teams move from insight to measurable impact.
